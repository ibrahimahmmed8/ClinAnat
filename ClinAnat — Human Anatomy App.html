Vertebral Level Reference
C3–C5 — Phrenic nerve
Origin of the phrenic nerve (C3,4,5 keeps the diaphragm alive). Injury above C3 causes apnoea.
T4/T5 — Carina, aortic arch
Tracheal bifurcation, sternal angle of Louis, 2nd rib junction, aortic arch beginning/ending.
T8 — Vena caval hiatus
IVC and right phrenic nerve pass through the diaphragm. Landmark for central line insertion.
T10 — Oesophageal hiatus
Oesophagus + vagal trunks. Hiatus hernia most common at this level.
T12 — Aortic hiatus
Descending aorta + thoracic duct + azygos vein pass posterior to the median arcuate ligament.
L1 — Transpyloric plane
Pylorus, fundus of gallbladder, duodenojejunal flexure, superior mesenteric artery origin, hilum of kidneys.
L4 — Bifurcation of aorta
Aorta bifurcates into common iliac arteries. Surface mark: level of iliac crests. CPR landmark.
Surface Anatomy Landmarks
Sternal angle (Angle of Louis)
T4/5; 2nd costal cartilage; bifurcation of trachea; division of superior/inferior mediastinum.
McBurney's Point
1/3 from ASIS to umbilicus. Base of appendix (tip varies). Maximum tenderness in appendicitis.
Murphy's Point
Right MCL at costal margin. Gallbladder fundus. Murphy's sign positive in acute cholecystitis.
Femoral triangle
Boundaries: inguinal ligament (superior), sartorius (lateral), adductor longus (medial). Contents: NAVEL — Nerve, Artery, Vein, Empty space, Lymphatics.
